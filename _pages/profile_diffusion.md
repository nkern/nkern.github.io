Score based generative models have become widespread in commercial and scientific applications. However, their reliable adoption within the sciences hinges on their ability to perform robust posterior density estimation. For example, diffusion models are known to memorize their training data under certain regimes, limiting their generalizability. Here we explore when and how these models transition from memorization to generalization when trained on cosmological datasets, and the impact this has on density estimation and parameter inference in scientific settings.
