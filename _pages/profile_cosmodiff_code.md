### cosmo_diffusion
<img src="../assets/img/square-github.svg" width="17" height="17">
<a href="https://github.com/nkern/cosmo_diffusion" target="_blank">nkern/cosmo_diffusion</a>

A self-contained framework for training, sampling from, and evaluating diffusion models for cosmological datasets of various types, built on top of pytorch and huggingface. Currently UNets and Transformer models are supported, with future support for graphs.
